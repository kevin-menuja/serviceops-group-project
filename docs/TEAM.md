- **Project:** ServiceOps — Field Service & Maintenance Management System
- **Qualification:** Higher Diploma in Computing & Software Engineering
- **Module:** Computing Project (CSE5015)
- **Semester:** 4th
- **Submission type:** Final group project

Our five-member group developed ServiceOps as the final project for the diploma. These names, roles, and responsibility descriptions are transcribed from the **ServiceOps Project Proposal Approval Sheet** provided by our group. ## Attribution and privacy

ServiceOps is a **collaborative academic submission**. Repository ownership and commit history must not be interpreted as a claim that a single member created the entire system. This document records our team roster and proposal-defined division of responsibilities.

The supplied approval-sheet images include academic student identification numbers. We deliberately exclude those numbers and the original approval-sheet screenshots from this public repository. All names and roles are documented without personal student identifiers.
